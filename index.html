02 / WHAT WE FOUND

The problem isn't small.

We spoke with students from schools, college seniors and our classmates to understand how they actually make career decisions.

50%

No clear direction

Had no idea what they wanted and were simply following trends or what others were doing.

30%

Some idea

Were influenced by trends, remote work or high-paying career expectations.

20%

Trying to learn

Had some direction and used YouTube, but had no way to connect with real professionals.
